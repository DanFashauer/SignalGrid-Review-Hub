# Company Operating Pack

This pack organizes founder, company, and strategic-path materials. It is not legal, financial, tax, employment, or valuation advice.

## Preferred path

Founder/CEO or retained product leadership path with creative/product-control preference, staged design-partner validation, and careful partner/investor conversations.

## Strategic paths

1. Founder-led design partner path.
2. Strategic investment path with product-control protections.
3. OEM/embedded partnership path.
4. Acquisition with retained product leadership.
5. Full buyout as last option.

## Operating principles

- Preserve product direction and public-safe validation.
- Avoid unsupported valuation, revenue, legal, or transaction claims.
- Keep proof deterministic until private-context validation is explicitly approved.
