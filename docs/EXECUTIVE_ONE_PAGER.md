# Executive One-Pager

SignalGrid is an operational trust orchestration layer for shared, mobile, and frontline environments. It connects identity, device posture, credential-reader events, custody, network context, workflow ownership, routing, audit evidence, and verification expectations into approval-gated trust decisions while existing enterprise systems remain systems of record.

## Who SignalGrid helps

SignalGrid is intended for teams evaluating shared-device, mobile, custody, frontline, and operational trust workflows, including:

- Healthcare and frontline operations teams that depend on shared devices, badge events, docks, lockers, and shift-based workflows.
- Identity, MDM, UEM, credential-reader, locker, workflow, and security vendors exploring orchestration opportunities around their existing systems.
- Design partners, strategic partners, investors, or potential acquirers reviewing whether a public-safe proof layer can become a private sandbox validation path.

## Problem SignalGrid solves

Shared, mobile, and frontline workflows often fail because no single source system sees the full context. IAM may know the user, MDM/UEM may know the device posture, credential readers may know badge events, lockers may know custody, network tools may know access path, ITSM/SOAR may know ownership, and audit systems may know evidence.

SignalGrid frames the missing layer as operational trust orchestration: normalize signals, decide outcomes, route approved actions, audit events, and verify expected results without claiming ownership of the source systems.

## Why shared/mobile/frontline environments need it

Shared and mobile environments create fast context changes:

- A user may badge into one zone, pick up a shared device, move through a workflow, dock or lock equipment, and hand off responsibility during a shift.
- A device may be compliant in one context but risky in another if custody, location, network, or workflow ownership does not match.
- Existing tools can be strong individually while still leaving gaps between identity, posture, custody, routing, approval, and audit evidence.

SignalGrid's public Review Hub demonstrates how those signals can be discussed in one deterministic review surface before any private sandbox or live integration work is considered.

## Current proof status

The current proof is public-safe, synthetic, and fixture-backed. Validated assets include the Review Hub, Connector Emulator Dashboard, Credential Reader / Smart Locker Dashboard narrative, Connector Emulator Smoke evidence, Autopilot Control Plane, and the Strategic Buyer / Partner Pitch Pack.

## What is synthetic today

Today, the proof uses deterministic fixtures and simulated evidence. Connector scenarios, Microsoft Graph sandbox proof, posture proof, credential-reader events, custody states, routing outcomes, and approval gates are represented without live customer systems, tenant data, credentials, PHI, PII, production integrations, or real vendor API calls.

## What is not claimed

SignalGrid Review Hub does not claim production readiness, compliance certification, regulatory approval, current vendor partnership, marketplace certification, vendor endorsement, acquisition outcome, valuation, legal outcome, financial promise, or autonomous production remediation. It does not replace IAM, MDM, UEM, PACS, SIEM, ITSM, credential-reader, locker, or other source systems. Live integrations require separate private/sandbox validation and owner approval.
