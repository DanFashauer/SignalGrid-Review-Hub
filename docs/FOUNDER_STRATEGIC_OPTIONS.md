# Founder Strategic Options

This document frames business-strategy options only. It is not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice and does not make valuation promises.

## Options

### Remain founder / CEO with strategic partner

Keep product control while using a strategic partner for domain validation, design-partner access, and channel feedback.

### Minority investment / design partner path

Use limited capital or design-partner support to validate the sandbox and pilot sequence while preserving founder control.

### OEM / embedded partnership path

Explore embedding SignalGrid trust orchestration into another vendor's workflow or device ecosystem while keeping clear source-system boundaries.

### Acquisition with retained product leadership

Consider strategic acquisition only if product leadership, public-safety discipline, and the operational trust orchestration thesis remain protected.

### Full buyout as last path

Treat a full buyout as a final option when creative-control requirements, product continuity, and founder outcomes are explicitly resolved.

## Founder / CEO control preference

The preferred path is strategic partnership, investment, or an acquisition structure with the founder remaining in CEO, product leadership, or creative-control role where possible. A full buyout is not the default preferred path, and this document makes no valuation, legal, financial, or transaction guarantee claims.

See [Founder Control Requirements](FOUNDER_CONTROL_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the working conversation filter.

## Creative-control requirements

- Preserve the orchestration thesis.
- Keep high-risk actions approval-gated.
- Avoid unsupported partnership, certification, or production claims.
- Protect design quality and end-user trust.

## Recommended next conversations

- Design partners in shared-device or frontline operations.
- Strategic partners in identity, endpoint, custody, network, and workflow categories.
- Advisors with healthcare IT, security operations, or enterprise mobility experience.

## Diligence package needed

- Validated proof commands and artifacts.
- Product thesis and demo guide.
- Public-safety guardrails.
- Backlog and staged real-world testing plan.
- Founder creative-control terms.
