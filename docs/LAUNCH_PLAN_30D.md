sentence, label hierarchy), strategy DR-012 (SMB/mid-market lean-IT segment,

## Standing rules for the whole push

- **Truth discipline is the sales pitch**: nothing in any outreach message,
  site page, or pilot doc may claim beyond the launch profile; the
  launch-claims gate + publication boundary enforce it mechanically where
  they can, and the outreach log makes the rest auditable.
- **The owner is touched only for**: taking calls, true consent gates, and a
  daily digest (what went out, what came back, what changed).
- **Halt word**: any instruction from the owner to stop outreach stops it
  immediately and completely.
- This plan reshapes on contact with reality; changes land as dated edits
  here, never silently.
