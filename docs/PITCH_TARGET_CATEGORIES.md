# Pitch Target Categories

This document groups potential strategic buyer, partner, investor, and design-partner audiences by category only. It does not claim current partnerships, endorsements, certifications, marketplace status, acquisition interest, or commercial commitments.

## Category map

| Category | Why the category may care | Public-safe conversation angle | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity security vendors | Identity systems know who the user or workload is, but may not see custody, shared-device, badge, zone, or workflow context together. | SignalGrid can demonstrate orchestration around identity as one signal among many. | Do not claim SignalGrid replaces IAM, IdP, IGA, or identity security platforms. |
| MDM/UEM vendors | Device posture and compliance matter in frontline workflows but may need physical custody and workflow context. | Show fixture-backed posture plus custody and approval-gate narratives. | Do not claim live MDM/UEM integrations or replacement of MDM/UEM. |
| Healthcare workflow/shared-device vendors | Shared clinical and frontline environments need auditable handoff, access, custody, and workflow continuity. | Use synthetic dashboards to discuss design-partner feedback and staged readiness. | Do not include PHI, PII, customer data, production readiness, or compliance claims. |
| Credential reader and smart locker hardware vendors | Readers, lockers, docks, and bays produce custody and presence events that can improve trust decisions. | Position SignalGrid as an orchestration/evidence layer around fixture-backed hardware event models. | Do not claim hardware certification, endorsement, or live integration support. |
| Network/security vendors | Network zone, access path, and segmentation context can influence trust decisions. | Discuss network context as a normalized signal in the connector emulator. | Do not claim live network enforcement or autonomous remediation. |
| ITSM/SOAR/workflow vendors | Workflow ownership, routing, approval gates, and evidence records are central to operational response. | Show approval-required routing and audit evidence as handoff points. | Do not claim SignalGrid replaces ITSM, SOAR, SIEM, or workflow systems. |
| Healthcare IT integrators | Integrators can evaluate staged readiness, public-safe demo flows, and implementation boundaries. | Ask for design feedback on private sandbox prerequisites. | Do not claim customer deployment, regulatory approval, or implementation readiness. |
| Non-human identity / identity governance companies | Devices, lockers, service identities, and shared endpoints create identity-adjacent trust questions. | Discuss governance inputs and evidence needs without production assertions. | Do not claim compliance attestation or governance replacement. |
| Frontline operations platforms | Frontline teams need reliable shared-device access, custody, escalation, and auditability. | Present SignalGrid as an orchestration concept for operational trust. | Do not claim unsupervised production remediation or operational replacement. |

## Prioritization lens

Prioritize conversations that can validate one of these questions without requiring live integration work:

1. Are the normalized signals the right ones for shared, mobile, and frontline trust decisions?
2. Are approval gates and ownership handoffs understandable to operators and reviewers?
3. Which sandbox-only signal should be tested first in a private, read-only setting?
4. Which category would benefit from an embedded/OEM orchestration layer rather than a standalone workflow?
5. Which founder-control structure would preserve product quality and public-safety discipline?
