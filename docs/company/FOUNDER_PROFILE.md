**Source.** The founder's résumé, shared with the cloud lane on 2026-09-12 with the
instruction that the assistant *"should be my assistant that thinks like me and should
know my responses and knowledge."* The document itself is owner-held (Google Drive,
DR-034's corpus); it is not committed, and its contact details are deliberately not
reproduced here. This tree is public.

**Why this file exists.** Until 2026-09-12 the owner-comms skill described the owner as
"a non-technical founder". That was wrong in a way that shaped every reply: he is not a
software developer, and he is one of the most technical people in the room on the
problem this company exists to solve. A role that translates *Intune* for him is
wasting his time; a role that assumes he cannot read a compliance policy is wrong.

## What follows for every role

1. **He is the ICP archetype.** The senior platform engineer who owns shared-device
   workflows in a hospital system is the buyer-side technical evaluator AND the operator
   who carries the pager. `docs/company/ICP_EVIDENCE.md` Finding 9 records what that
   does and does not establish.
2. **UEM, MDM and identity vocabulary is HIS, never translated down.** Intune compliance
   policies, Workspace ONE smart groups, Jamf PreStage, Entra Conditional Access,
   supervised devices, Apple Business Manager, ServiceNow incidents and changes — say
   them plainly. What gets translated is developer vocabulary: TypeScript, worktrees,
   mutation guards, CI gates, manifests. The owner-comms skill carries the rule.
3. **His standards are the repository's standards, arrived at from the operations side.**
   Root-cause analysis, validate a change before broader production use, technical
   documentation and operational standards, cross-functional coordination, vendor
   collaboration. "Numbers come from output, never memory" is how a platform engineer
   troubleshoots; the gates are that habit made mechanical.
4. **He decides; roles execute — and his pushback is a decision.** DR-037, DR-040 and
   DR-039 were each a correction of a posture that was too cautious for him. When he
   says "I want this", the work is to land it in the form that keeps the hard lines,
   and to say in one line which line shaped the form.
5. **Ask him the domain question; do not guess it.** How a real ward hands a device
   between shifts, what Intune actually does when a compliance policy flips, which
   ServiceNow field the on-call engineer reads first — he knows. A role that invents
   the answer is slower than one that asks.
6. **The absorption bar is his (DR-039).** A resource he shares is taken if any part of
   it can aid building any aspect of the company; the only exclusions are licence,
   auto-execution, egress without his consent, and directory collision.
