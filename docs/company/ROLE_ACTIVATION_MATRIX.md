# SignalGrid — role activation matrix

Current as of August 21, 2026.

This matrix is the gap between the company SignalGrid is designed to become
([ORG_STRUCTURE.md](ORG_STRUCTURE.md), [ROLE_CATALOG.md](ROLE_CATALOG.md)) and
the company that exists today. Today's payroll is one person: Dan Fashauer,
founder and CEO. There are no employees, no contractors on recurring terms, and
no fractional professionals engaged. Every other row below is either held by the
founder, held by an AI agent lane under founder review, enforced mechanically by
a gate in this repository, or not held at all.

Role is not employee. A row existing here is a statement that the work has an
owner or a trigger, not that anyone has been hired.

## The four statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **ACTIVE** | Performed now as a standing job, by the founder or by an agent lane running continuously. |
| **COVERED** | The responsibility is live and something named holds it — the founder, one or more agent lanes, or a mechanical gate — but no person is employed in the role. Coverage is stated with its holes. |
| **FRACTIONAL** | Designed to be filled by an outside professional engaged for the need. None is engaged today; the row records the terms of engagement, not a relationship. |
| **FUTURE** | Not held, and not needed yet. The activation trigger is the condition that turns the role on. |

## Reading this matrix

- **A FUTURE role with a clear trigger is a plan, not a vacancy.** The trigger is
  the observable condition that turns the role on. Until it fires, the row is
  correct as written: the work does not exist yet.
- **A COVERED role names its covering mechanism.** Coverage is a person, a named
  agent lane, or a gate that fails the build — never an intention. Where coverage
  has holes, the row states them, because a stated hole is cheaper than a
  discovered one.
- **A FRACTIONAL role records terms, not a relationship.** No fractional
  professional — CFO, counsel, tax, recruiter, penetration tester, auditor — is
  engaged today.
- **Nothing here is a headcount commitment.** No hiring decision, budget, offer,
  or timeline follows from a row appearing in this matrix. Role is not employee,
  capability is not headcount, and the future organization is not the current
  payroll.
- **Agent lanes are not staff.** A lane is software the founder runs and
  ratifies; accountability for every lane's output stays with the founder.
