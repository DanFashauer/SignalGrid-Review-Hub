SignalGrid Competitive Battlecard · Internal

The decision layer for the moment a workflow fires on a shared device — is the person holding it right now allowed to do this?

How to use this card: we win by being narrow and true, not by claiming everything. Anchor every conversation on the three pillars below, concede where an incumbent already wins, and reframe competitors as signal sources — never rip-and-replace. Never overclaim custody certainty, compliance, or novelty on “step-up.” The custody, baseline and workflow-risk families are deferred — proven in the public repository, not shipping at Limited GA — so they are roadmap in every conversation, never a current capability.

01

The moat — always anchor here

Signal others don't fuse — roadmap

Custody & tamper — a design target

Is the shared device docked, charging, in-hand, or tampered — and is the badged-in holder still the one using it? Others treat charge/dock state as logout hygiene. Making it a gating input is where this is going, and it is deferred: modeled and proven in the public repository, not evaluated at Limited GA. Sell the direction, never the tense.

Signal others don't fuse

CIS baseline drift

Not “is the device enrolled,” but “has it drifted from its security baseline” — evaluated at the moment of action, as a weighted input to the decision.

Architecture others won't adopt

Vendor-neutral overlay PDP

One fused allow / step-up / restrict / deny verdict and one audit plane across a mixed IAM / badge / UEM / EDR / ITSM stack. We consume their signals; we never own the login.

If a deal touches none of these three, it isn't our wedge — qualify hard.

Competitor plays

OLOIDThreat · High

The closest thesis-twin

They own — concede it
Passwordless frontline login + badge attribution, HIPAA / 21 CFR Part 11 story, real logos, hardware. Their FIL (May 2026) is moving toward per-action governance.
Our wedge
They attribute at login; we decide per action — and we treat how current the device answer is as a first-class input, so a stale “compliant” tightens the verdict instead of passing as green. FIL routes to a human; we compute a fail-closed verdict with reproducible evidence attached.
“OLOID already does this” → say
“OLOID resolves who’s logged in, brilliantly. We decide whether this action should proceed given the device’s compliance, how current that answer is, and whether the device can vouch for itself right now — and their badge is an input we can consume as that family ships.”
Walk / partner when
The whole ask is passwordless login + attribution. Offer coexistence.
ImprivataHigh · Med

Healthcare incumbent — the hardest wall

They own — concede loudly
Badge tap-in/out, roaming, Entra SSO, per-transaction EPCS step-up, posture checks, charge-state custody, and Verosint (Nov 2025) for runtime risk. “We already own the badge” is largely true.
Our wedge
A neutral, unified per-action verdict — device compliance + its freshness + the device’s present authority — into one decision + one audit plane across a mixed fleet, consuming their badge rather than replacing it. (Custody, baseline and workflow-risk families are deferred: proven in the public repository, not shipping at Limited GA.)
“Isn't this just Imprivata?” → say
“All-Imprivata in one hospital? They cover most of it. We're for the mixed fleet and non-EPCS workflows, and we sit above Imprivata — taking its badge as one input.”
Do NOT fight
Badge-SSO, roaming, Entra SSO, EPCS signing. Best ground: retail / warehouse / logistics, where they're shallow.

04

Where we win / where we walk

Win when

  • Mixed, multi-vendor frontline stack needing one decision + audit plane
  • Retail / warehouse / logistics frontline — incumbents are shallow here
  • Per-action gating wanted without changing how workers log in
  • Custody/tamper or baseline-drift is a felt pain — qualify for it, but sell it as roadmap: those families are deferred
  • Audit must explain why an action was allowed, not just who did it

Walk or partner when

  • Pure “kill shared passwords / passwordless login” ask → OLOID's
  • Deep all-Imprivata healthcare shop wanting only badge-SSO + EPCS
  • Buyer wants to replace their IdP / badge system — we're an overlay
  • Infrastructure / server / engineer access → Teleport's; refer
  • Compliance satisfied by attribution alone — no “why” needed
05

Objection handling

Isn't this just Imprivata / OLOID?
Those own the login and badge. We own the decision at the moment of action, across your whole stack — and we take their badge as an input. A layer above, not a replacement.
We already have Entra Conditional Access.
Great — we consume it. Entra decides the session; we decide the action on a shared device, using device compliance, how current that answer is, and the device's present authority. (Custody and baseline deepen that later — deferred, not shipping at Limited GA.)
We already do step-up for controlled substances.
EPCS is mandated and solved. We're for the other high-risk workflows and the mixed-fleet fusion + one audit trail — not EPCS re-invention.
Sounds like a policy engine / PDP.
It is one — for the physical frontline. The novelty is the signals (custody, tamper, badge-holder, CIS drift), not the pattern.
How reliable is “custody” really?
It's one weighted evidence input, never ground truth. We publish false-accept / false-reject behavior and let policy decide how much it counts.
Is this production-ready / certified?
Today it's a fixture-backed, public-safe decision core with real integration adapters gated behind explicit config. Not compliance-certified, doesn't replace source systems. Here's exactly what it decides today.
06

Discovery & disqualify

Ask early

  • When a worker does a high-risk action on a shared device, what decides if it proceeds right then — and is it the same across every app?
  • Who was physically holding the device at that moment, and how do you know?
  • How many vendors touch that decision — and is there one place that fuses them into a verdict and one audit trail?
  • Do you evaluate baseline drift at the moment of action, or only at enrollment?
  • Outside healthcare — what's watching shared-scanner workflows in your retail/warehouse sites?

Disqualifiers

  • Ask = passwordless login / kill shared passwords → OLOID's
  • Single-vendor all-Imprivata shop, badge-SSO + EPCS only
  • Infrastructure / engineer access → Teleport's
  • Wants to replace IdP / badge / UEM — we're an overlay
  • Compliance met by attribution alone — thin value; requalify
Leave this in every prospect's head

You've already bought identity, badges, and device management — SignalGrid is the neutral access-decision service that fuses them into one allow / step-up / deny verdict at the moment a shared-device workflow fires — with an audit trail that explains why.
