# Founder Control Requirements

This document records founder-control preferences for SignalGrid strategic conversations. It is a planning guide only and is not legal, financial, tax, employment, investment, acquisition, or governance advice.

## Preferred structure

The preferred strategic path preserves the founder's CEO, product leadership, or creative-control role where possible. Strategic partnership, design partnership, investment, OEM/embedded collaboration, or acquisition with retained product leadership are preferred over an immediate full buyout.

## Control requirements

- Preserve the product thesis as `docs/PURPOSE.md` §2 states it: SignalGrid connects the systems a building already runs into one grid that decides and acts on the person's behalf across door, device, room and app; no category label is ratified (DR-019/DR-020).
- Preserve product quality, user experience, and evidence discipline.
- Keep high-risk actions simulated, constrained, or approval-required unless separately validated under private/sandbox controls.
- Keep existing enterprise systems as systems of record.
- Maintain public-safe documentation boundaries and protected core boundaries.
- Avoid unsupported production, compliance, certification, partnership, replacement, autonomous remediation, valuation, legal, financial, or acquisition claims.
- Treat a full buyout as a last option, not the default preferred path.

## Conversation filter

A strategic conversation is better aligned when it can answer yes to most of these questions:

1. Can the founder keep meaningful CEO, product, or creative leadership influence?
2. Can SignalGrid's orchestration role remain distinct from source systems of record?
3. Can validation proceed through staged public-safe and private-sandbox gates?
4. Can the partner or buyer support approval-gated, auditable, non-hype product development?
5. Can public claims remain conservative until private validation supports stronger statements?

## Non-claims

These requirements do not guarantee any transaction, employment role, governance structure, financial outcome, valuation, or legal term.
