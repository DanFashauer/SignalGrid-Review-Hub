# Founder Role and Control Strategy

## Preference

The preferred posture is founder/CEO or retained product leadership with creative and product control. Strategic conversations should test whether partners, investors, or acquirers respect the product thesis and staged validation path.

## Non-negotiable discussion points

- Product story stays centered on signal normalization, explainable decisions, approval routing, audit evidence, and verification.
- Existing enterprise systems remain systems of record.
- Public claims remain conservative and evidence-backed.
- Any private-context testing requires explicit approval and separate guardrails.
