# Fundraising & outcome options — the map

**Public-safe planning doc. Not legal or financial advice.** This lays out where
SignalGrid is today, the realistic ways to fund it, and the sequence that keeps
the best outcomes open. It is a founder planning aid, not a claim of traction,
funding, or any existing relationship with any company named here.

## Where SignalGrid honestly is today

- **Stage:** pre-production concept with working, deterministic **review
  artifacts** and **clickable demos** — not a shipping product, no paying
  customers, no design partner live yet.
- **Strongest assets:** the live demos (room-entry console, the embedded clinical
  flow, the macOS/Windows desktop flow), a deterministic decision core with
  proofs, honest readiness docs, and a clear category ("context-aware trust /
  orchestration layer that sits **above** IAM / badge / UEM / EDR").
- **What's missing for money on good terms:** (1) a **design partner** using it,
  (2) **evidence it works** in a real setting, (3) **IP protection** (provisional
  patent) before deep disclosure.

## The sequence (do NOT sell too early)

The counter-intuitive truth: the way to earn the *investment or acquisition* you
want is to not ask for it first. Build leverage, then negotiate.

1. **Protect the idea** — provisional patent on the method + trademark the name,
   before broad disclosure to anyone who could build it. (Owner-gated item in
   `BUILD_BACKLOG.md`.)
2. **Get one design partner** — a single frontline org (hospital ward, warehouse,
   data-center/NOC) willing to try it on a small, low-risk pilot.
3. **Show it works** — a short pilot with real (de-identified) signal, measured.
4. **Then raise / negotiate** — with a partner + evidence + protected IP, both
   investment and acquisition happen on *your* terms, not at a fire-sale price.

Skipping to step 4 at concept stage sets the price low and risks giving away the
idea for little.

## Outcome paths (and the honest read on each)

| Path | Realistic today? | Notes |
| ---- | ---------------- | ----- |
| **Design partner / paid pilot** | ✅ Best first move | De-risks everything else; non-dilutive; creates the evidence investors want. |
| **Accelerator + angels (pre-seed)** | ✅ Realistic | Small check + network + credibility. The normal first money for a concept. |
| **Seed VC round** | ⚠️ After traction | Warm intros only; needs a partner/pilot to be fundable. |
| **Equity crowdfunding** | ⚠️ Later | Literally "open for investors," but real legal/compliance overhead; better after traction. |
| **Strategic investment / acquisition (e.g. an incumbent)** | ⚠️ After leverage | Most appealing, least likely at concept stage, and worst-priced if done now. Aim for it *after* steps 1–3. |
| **"Absorbed, keep a board seat, run the mission"** | ❌ Unlikely now | Board seats follow leverage. At concept stage this usually becomes an employee/advisor role on a small deal. Earn the seat with traction. |

## Where to "open it for investors" — by fit

Most-fit first for a solo, pre-seed founder:

### 1. Accelerators (recommended first move)
Give a small check, a warm-intro network, and credibility.
- **General / enterprise:** Y Combinator, Techstars, AlchemistX (enterprise B2B).
- **Healthcare:** Cedars-Sinai Accelerator (Techstars), MedTech Innovator.
- **Security:** DataTribe, MACH37, Forgepoint's founder programs.
- Apply via each program's site and via **F6S** (aggregates accelerator apps).

### 2. Angel / discovery platforms
List, get discovered, take angel checks.
- **Wellfound (AngelList)** — profile + raise + syndicates.
- **Gust**, **Crunchbase** (visibility), **PitchBook** (research only).

### 3. Equity crowdfunding ("open for investors," literally)
A public raise under Reg CF. Powerful reach; real legal/compliance overhead;
better *after* some traction.
- **Wefunder**, **Republic**, **StartEngine**.

### 4. Seed VCs to research (warm intro, never cold-blast)
Health + security specialists and generalists that look at this space. Research
fit and current stage before reaching out; do not assume any will fund:
- **Health-security:** .406 Ventures, Flare Capital.
- **Cyber seed:** Ten Eleven Ventures, YL Ventures, Forgepoint.
- **Generalists w/ health & security:** General Catalyst, Bessemer.

### 5. Non-dilutive (money that costs no equity)
- A **paid pilot** with a health system or logistics operator.
- Health-innovation grants; **NSF SBIR** (deep-tech non-dilutive).

## Controlled disclosure checklist (protect IP while you pitch)

Before sending material to anyone — especially a company that could build this:

- [ ] Provisional patent filed (or consciously deferred) **before** deep detail.
- [ ] Trademark on the name in progress.
- [ ] Share tier decided per audience:
  - **Public:** vision + a live demo + the "sits above your stack" positioning.
  - **NDA only:** architecture, the core decision method, code.
- [ ] For an adjacent player: frame as **complementary** (we consume your signals,
      we don't replace your login), never as a head-on replacement.
- [ ] No packet claims an existing relationship, certification, or production
      status that isn't real (keeps you honest *and* out of legal trouble).
