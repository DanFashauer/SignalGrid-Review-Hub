# Investor / Design-Partner Readiness

SignalGrid v0.2 readiness is the transition from public-safe proof assets to a focused product foundation. The story should be narrow: frontline and shared-device teams need a runtime trust decision before a workflow continues.

## Package requirements

- Pitch deck focused on the painful workflow, decision loop, Microsoft first path, tenant/security foundation, pilot criteria, and roadmap.
- Executive summary that states current synthetic proof boundaries.
- Demo script using fixture-backed Review Hub evidence only.
- Product screenshots from current public-safe dashboards or future local QA captures.
- Roadmap from functional alpha to investor/design-partner ready, paid pilot ready, and production SaaS ready.
- Security posture summary covering tenant boundaries, RBAC, audit, rate limiting, input validation, PII-safe logging, and secret handling expectations.
- Design-partner pilot plan with success criteria and non-goals.
- Data room index pointing to public-safe documents, validation evidence, and diligence answers.
- Diligence checklist for technical, security, product, pilot, and business review.

## Public-safe narrative

SignalGrid is an operational trust control plane for frontline, shared-device, and mobile workflows. It normalizes signals from systems of record, decides an outcome, routes approved actions, audits evidence, and verifies expected results. It does not replace systems of record and does not claim production readiness, compliance certification, vendor endorsement, or unsupervised production remediation.
