# Partnership and Acquisition Paths

This document frames strategic paths for SignalGrid conversations. It is not legal, financial, tax, investment, valuation, acquisition, or transaction advice.

## Preferred order of exploration

1. Strategic partnership with founder/product control preserved.
2. Design partner path for workflow validation.
3. Strategic investment path with disciplined staged validation.
4. OEM / embedded partner path where source-system boundaries stay clear.
5. Acquisition with founder in CEO, product, or creative leadership where possible.
6. Full buyout only as a last option.

## Path details

### Design partner

Goal: validate workflow fit, signal usefulness, evidence needs, and staged readiness.

Use public-safe Review Hub dashboards and synthetic proof assets only. Any later private sandbox step needs owner approval, safe test boundaries, and no public claim of production support.

### OEM / embedded partner

Goal: evaluate whether SignalGrid's orchestration model can be embedded into another vendor's shared-device, custody, endpoint, identity, or workflow product experience.

The embedded model should preserve existing systems as systems of record and avoid claims that SignalGrid replaces IAM, MDM/UEM, credential-reader, locker, PACS, SIEM, ITSM, SOAR, or network platforms.

### Strategic investment

Goal: fund or support private validation while preserving founder/product leadership and public-safety discipline.

Use validated proof assets, risk lanes, backlog, and staged readiness. Do not include valuation promises, return projections, legal claims, or financial guarantees in public Review Hub materials.

### Acquisition with founder/product leadership

Goal: combine SignalGrid's orchestration thesis with a strategic platform while preserving founder leadership where possible.

Evaluation criteria should include product continuity, public-safety posture, approval-gate integrity, protected core boundaries, and whether the founder can continue leading product direction or creative execution.

### Full buyout

Goal: owner-controlled last option only.

A full buyout is not the default preferred path. It should be considered only if founder-control requirements, product continuity, and post-transaction stewardship are explicitly resolved outside this public repository.

## Non-claims

This document does not claim active negotiations, buyer interest, partnership status, acquisition status, certification, production readiness, valuation, or a guaranteed transaction path.
