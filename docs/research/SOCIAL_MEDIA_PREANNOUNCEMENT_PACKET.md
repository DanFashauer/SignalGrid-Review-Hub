# Social Media Pre-Announcement Packet

This packet helps the founder begin public category-building for SignalGrid without treating the work as a product launch, partnership announcement, funding announcement, customer announcement, production-readiness claim, certification claim, or compliance claim.

## Messaging posture

Use this posture for public social content:

- **Pre-announcement:** signal that something important is coming without implying availability, deployment, customer use, funding, partnership, or acquisition activity.
- **Category education:** teach the gap between fragmented enterprise systems and operational trust decisions in shared, mobile, and frontline environments.
- **Founder POV:** write from the founder's perspective with direct, serious, enterprise-grade language.
- **Market problem discovery:** invite comparison notes, design-partner conversations, and problem validation.
- **Not a production launch:** do not imply that SignalGrid is generally available, production-ready, certified, compliant, or customer-proven.
- **Not a partnership announcement:** do not name or imply current vendor partnerships, marketplace status, endorsement, or alliance.
- **Not a funding announcement:** do not imply investment, valuation, financial commitment, acquisition, or transaction status.
- **Not a customer announcement:** do not imply customer deployment, customer proof, PHI/PII access, tenant data, or live production integrations.

## Core public message

SignalGrid is exploring operational trust orchestration for shared, mobile, and frontline environments.

The category thesis:

- Identity alone is not enough.
- Device posture alone is not enough.
- Custody alone is not enough.
- Network and security signals alone are not enough.
- Workflow ownership alone is not enough.

Enterprises need auditable decisions that connect identity, device posture, custody, operational context, workflow ownership, approval gates, and verification expectations while existing systems remain systems of record.

## Anchor message

Something important is coming for shared-device, mobile, and frontline enterprise environments.

Identity tells you who someone is. MDM tells you something about the device. Credential readers and lockers tell you custody. Networks tell you where traffic flows. ITSM tells you who owns the response.

But nobody consistently answers: given all of that, what should happen next?

That is the problem SignalGrid is exploring.

## Two-week posting sequence

| Timing | Post type | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Founder teaser | Introduce that something important is coming and name the operational question. |
| Day 3 | Problem post | Teach that enterprise systems know fragments of the truth. |
| Day 5 | Shared-device/frontline post | Explain why shared devices are sessions, identities, custody events, and workflows. |
| Day 7 | Architecture post | Show how IAM, MDM/UEM, credential readers, lockers, network tools, ITSM, and SIEM see different parts of the picture. |
| Day 10 | Partner-category post | Invite partners that already own identity, device, custody, workflow, or security layers to compare notes. |
| Day 14 | Soft CTA | Ask for design-partner conversations and problem validation, not sales calls. |

## CTA options

- "If this problem sounds familiar, I’d like to compare notes."
- "If you work in shared devices, identity, UEM, smart lockers, or frontline operations, I’d like to talk."
- "I’m looking for design-partner conversations, not a sales pitch."
- "More soon."

## Founder tone

Keep the tone serious, founder-led, enterprise-grade, confident, and plain-spoken. Avoid hype-heavy claims, buzzword stuffing, exaggerated urgency, and any implication that the public Review Hub is a production product.
