SignalGrid Trusted Room Entry · runs on-device

Context-aware trust & orchestration — Phase 1

A synthetic holder with a managed shared device approaches a space — a hospital room, a warehouse zone, or a fleet vehicle. SignalGrid runs the real decision core over identity, device posture, custody, badge binding, security baseline and workflow risk — then orchestrates the downstream actions for that vertical. Sensitive steps (a controlled-room door and PHI display, a high-value cage, or a cross-region cargo seal) are never automatic; they wait for a clinician, supervisor, or dispatcher to confirm. Everything here runs in this browser tab — no server, no network. Scope: this demo exercises the full signal fabric; custody, badge binding, baseline and workflow-risk families are deferred — proven in the repository, not shipping at Limited GA, where the three shipping signals are device compliance, its freshness, and the device’s authority to act now.
